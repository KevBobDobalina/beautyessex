The Allure & Philosophy

Beauty & Essex in Las Vegas transcends the conventional; it's a destination marrying glamour, sophistication, and culinary innovation. Its "jewel-box" interior sets a tone of discovery and allure, reflecting a dual identity of aesthetic luxury and classic culinary roots.

The dining philosophy is profoundly experiential and communal. Menu categories like "Jewels on Toast" and "Shareables," alongside signature items like "Beauty's Wonder Wheel," emphasize shared, interactive moments. It's a collective journey of flavors, fostering an engaging social atmosphere.

This guide proposes a culinary journey leveraging these unique strengths, ensuring an unforgettable evening for the discerning diner—as much about shared experience as exquisite flavors.

The Culinary Journey: A Three-Act Experience

The proposed meal is a progressive tasting experience, designed around Beauty & Essex's shareable concept. This approach maximizes exploration of diverse flavors and culinary creativity.

Act I: The Overture – Starters & Small Plates

Awaken the palate with fresh, vibrant flavors and textural contrasts, setting an elegant yet intriguing tone. Designed for sharing.

Tuna Poke Wonton Tacos ($23)

Chiffonade cilantro, radish, wasabi kewpie.

Visually stunning, texturally exciting. Crisp wonton, tender tuna, creamy spicy kick. Signals innovative spirit.

Grilled Cheese & Tomato Soup Dumplings ($21)

Smoked bacon.

Iconic. Fusion of comfort food & refined technique. Rich savory soup, delicate dumpling, smoky bacon. Playful yet sophisticated.

Yellowtail Crudo ($29)

Crispy garlic, rice pearls, pickled chilies, avocado mousse, tamari soy, sesame chili oil.

Delicate flavors, intricate textures. Buttery yellowtail, crunch, creaminess, subtle heat, umami. Highlights quality ingredients.

Act II: The Main Event – Entrées & Complements

Substantial, flavorful dishes as the centerpiece, showcasing prowess with larger proteins and elevated classics. Ideal for sharing.

Beef Wellington ($64)

Filet mignon, foie gras mousse, mushroom duxelles, red wine sauce.

Culinary masterpiece, statement piece. Premium filet, rich foie gras, earthy mushrooms, perfect pastry. Classic, refined indulgence.

Gochujang Salmon ($39)

Kimchi slaw, black garlic risotto, ginger aioli, furikake.

Vibrant, flavorful, lighter alternative. Spicy-sweet glaze, tangy slaw, umami risotto. Crucial balance and variety.

Truffle Mac & Cheese ($22, +$15 Lobster)

Truffle butter, fontina & white cheddar mornay, crispy Reggianito.

Epitome of decadent comfort. Aromatic truffle elevates a classic. Complements main courses.

Fried Brussels Sprouts ($15)

Sweet teardrop peppers, sherry vinegar, Fresno aioli.

Necessary textural contrast, refreshing acidity. Sophisticated tang and brightness. Keeps palate engaged.

Act III: The Sweet Encore – Desserts

A memorable and visually spectacular conclusion, offering playful innovation and classic comfort, designed for sharing.

Beauty's Wonder Wheel ($38)

Chef's selection for two or more people.

🎡

Quintessential B&E dessert. Grand finale, aligns with social dining. Variety, creativity, surprise. Interactive, visually stunning. Embodies shared indulgence and playful presentation.

Pinot Noir, Freeman (Russian River, 2022) - $26/glass: Lighter-bodied red. Harmonious with Gochujang Salmon.
